# Testing Gli1Cre efficiency in lung fibroblasts
2026-07-08

[Gli1Cre Efficiency test](https://github.com/maiaj58/tfcb_2026_demo/issues/1#issue-5824843401)

## Procedure
1. Cross Gli1CreER/Gli1CreER mouse to Ai14/Ai14 mouse to obtain the Gli1CreER/+ Ai14/+ genotype.
2. Treat 2 mice with 1mg tamoxifen (dissolved in corn oil) intraperitoneally for 4 treatments over the course of 9 days.
3. Leave 2 mice untreated and housed separately.
4. Wait 1 week after treatments, then sacrifice mice.
5. Dissect open the chest, cut the ascending aorta, then perfuse with 10mL PBS through the left ventricle.
6. Remove both lungs into a buffer containing 50 µg/ml Liberase TM and 25 µg/ml DNase I in pre-warmed RPMI-1640.
7. Blend lungs and incubate for 30 min at 37°C.
8. Filter homogenate through 100uM filter, then add 10mL 1x PBS.
9. Spin down tubes at 500g for 5 minutes.
10. Remove supernatant and stain with 200uL antibody cocktail for 30 minutes.
11. Spin tubes again at 500g for 5 minutes, remove supernatant.
12. Add PBS with DAPI at 1:1000 concentration, then run samples on flow cytometer.
